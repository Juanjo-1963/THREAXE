Threaxe

A rigorous programme.
Not another course.

Most executives don't have a time problem. They have a condition problem. Decisions pile up. Attention fractures. The agenda fills itself. And somewhere in the middle of all that, the things that actually matter get pushed to next week.

Threaxe works with directors and senior leaders who operate under sustained pressure and want to change how they perform — not just how they plan. Built on evidence, not on theory, and designed to produce results that last beyond the final session.

01 · State
State
You can't manage time well from a depleted state. Performance starts here — before the calendar, before the to-do list.
02 · Priority
Priority

Focus
